CELESTE ROSE – Jewellery Care Guide

To keep your jewellery looking beautiful, follow these basic care instructions:

1. Keep jewellery away from perfume.
2. Avoid exposing jewellery to sweat.
3. Keep jewellery away from water.
4. Do not leave jewellery exposed in open air.
5. Store your jewellery safely in a jewellery box or a plastic bag when not in use.

A little care and proper storage can help maintain the appearance of your jewellery.
